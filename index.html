A clearer money picture.

Capture your spending, understand where it goes, and make a plan that fits your life.

Keep the everyday things together.

Log expenses and income, set category budgets, record recurring subscriptions, and keep track of savings goals and money owed. Import CSV or supported PDF statements and export a portable backup.

Goldengo works with records you enter or import. It does not connect to your bank, move money, execute investments, or provide financial advice.

Private by design.

Your financial records live on your device, with optional syncing through your private iCloud database when available. Goldengo has no developer-operated account service, advertising, or analytics SDK.

For iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later.
