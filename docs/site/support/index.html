Contact support

Email lekacoding@gmail.com with your app version, iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and the steps that reproduce the problem.

Please do not send real bank statements, passwords, account numbers, financial backups, or screenshots containing private balances. A redacted screenshot or a small synthetic example is enough.

Do I need to link a bank?

No. Goldengo uses manual entries and statement imports. It does not log in to your bank, execute payments, or place trades.

Which imports are supported?

Use the app’s import flow for CSV and supported text-based PDF statements. You can review the detected columns and entries before saving. Not every bank’s PDF format is supported. Duplicate detection helps avoid importing the same entry twice.

How do I back up or restore?

Open Settings → Your data → Export financial data and save the CSV somewhere private. To restore a Goldengo backup, choose Restore Goldengo backup. Restore merges supported records with your existing data; it does not replace or erase newer entries.

Why does my widget or iCloud sync differ?

Check Storage & sync in Goldengo Settings. Widgets require the shared device store; iCloud sync also requires an available iCloud account and internet connection. Local entries remain useful offline. Allow time for iCloud to synchronize, and keep a private exported backup before making storage changes.

Can I use other currencies?

Set your default currency in Settings. Conversion uses public rates from ExchangeRate-API. Displayed conversions are estimates and may use cached rates; they are not a bank’s settlement rate.

How do I remove my data?

Removing an expense hides it from your app and totals but can retain an archived record for sync and undo. For full removal, remove the app from your devices and manage any remaining Goldengo iCloud data and backups in Apple’s storage settings. See the privacy policy for details.

Scope

Goldengo is a personal record-keeping tool, not financial advice. It is not affiliated with Apple or a bank. You remain responsible for checking imported amounts, due dates, and exchange-rate estimates.
