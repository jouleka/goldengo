Your records.
Your privacy.

This policy describes the Goldengo iOS app, developed by Jurgen Leka. Goldengo is a personal spending journal; it does not operate a bank or hold your funds.

Financial records stay with you.

Expenses, income, account labels, balances, budgets, goals, subscriptions, notes, and records of money owed are stored in the app’s local database. You choose what to enter. Goldengo does not send these financial records to the developer, an analytics service, an advertising network, or a bank-login service. There is no separate Goldengo account.

Private iCloud sync.

When available, Goldengo uses Apple’s CloudKit to synchronize records through your private iCloud database. Apple processes that data under its own iCloud terms and privacy policy. The developer cannot browse your private iCloud financial records. If iCloud is unavailable, the app can use local storage. Your system backup settings may also include app data in device backups.

Statements and receipts.

CSV and PDF statement parsing and receipt text recognition run on your device. Importing a statement does not upload it to a Goldengo server. Camera access is requested only when you choose to scan a receipt. Goldengo does not need access to your entire photo library.

Currency rates.

The app requests public currency rates from ExchangeRate-API at open.er-api.com. Requests contain no financial records, balances, statements, or Goldengo account identifier. The provider receives connection information such as your IP address and may retain it in operational logs and for abuse prevention and rate limiting. Goldengo treats this as diagnostic data used for app functionality, without advertising or tracking. Its processing is covered by the provider’s terms and privacy policy. Rates are cached on the device and may be unavailable or out of date.

Permissions, widgets and shortcuts.

Face ID or device authentication is handled by Apple’s authentication system; Goldengo does not receive biometric data. Optional reminders use local notifications. The widget and App Shortcuts share relevant app data through an on-device App Group. Widget amounts remain hidden on the Lock Screen unless you enable them in Settings. You can change notification and camera access in iOS Settings and app-lock and widget preferences within Goldengo.

Exports and support.

When you export a backup, you choose its recipient or destination using the system share sheet. Exported CSV files contain financial information; store and share them carefully. Goldengo removes the temporary app-created export after the share sheet closes. A copy you save or send remains with the destination you chose.

If you contact lekacoding@gmail.com, your email address and the information you send are processed to respond to your request. Please use synthetic examples and do not email bank statements, account credentials, or financial backups. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and related follow-up.

Retention and deletion.

App records remain until you remove them or the app’s storage. Removing an expense hides it from the app and totals; its archived record can remain in the database so the removal synchronizes across devices and can be undone. Uninstalling removes the app’s own local storage, but shared widget data, device backups, or private iCloud data may remain.

To remove all remaining copies, remove Goldengo from all of your devices and manage its data and backups in your Apple iCloud storage settings. If Goldengo data appears there, delete it there as well. Ask support if you need help identifying these controls. The developer cannot delete records inside your private iCloud database on your behalf. You may ask support to delete correspondence you previously sent.

No advertising or tracking.

Goldengo contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, cross-app tracking, or sale of personal data. This informational website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process basic web-server connection information under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes and contact.

This page will show the effective date of any updated policy. For privacy questions, contact Jurgen Leka at lekacoding@gmail.com.
