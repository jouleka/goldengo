## Pocket Truth — the lock screen claims what's in his pocket

**Pitch:** The lock screen permanently asserts what the books claim is physically on him, per currency: "7 500 L · 60 €." On cash-silent days the claim honestly fogs — "~7 500 ±1 400 · losing track since Tue" — the machine confessing its own blindness, never his lapse. One tap lands on the already-shipped type-the-balance numpad; he types what's actually there, the fog snaps to ±0 with the drop haptic, and the gap arrives pre-explained — "−800 · probably the Tuesday market run" — one tap books it as a real reversible entry, one flick leaves an honest Unaccounted. It never asks anything; it asserts, and reality argues back.

**Why him:** Cash is the un-audited half of his EUR+ALL dual life, and he physically holds ground truth dozens of times a day — every cash payment is a free, involuntary audit of the claim, zero willpower. A claim invites his inner auditor the way a summary never will: 'the books are wrong' is his only proven engagement spike (the double-drain rant), and fog is un-truth made visible — he cannot leave it alone, and only he can burn it off, which makes reconciliation a power move instead of a chore. The only verbs offered are his favorites: type one number, judge one guess. Never prompts, never re-asks, never asks him to count (the shipped note counter waits if he wants it).

**Daily pull:** 9am first pickup: "7 500 L · 60 € · even since Tue" — half a second, nothing to do. 2pm cash lunch via rhythm ghost: by the time the phone is pocketed the line has already moved — glance confirms books track hands. Market day, wallet out, widget says ±1 500: that is the itch — tap, type the real number, watch fog snap to zero and the gap explain itself. 11pm StandBy shows it big. What it returns: a permanently trustworthy answer to 'how much cash am I actually carrying' — a number he currently keeps in his head — plus daily proof the ledger tracks reality, the only thing he demonstrably cares about.

**Build sketch:** accessoryInline + accessoryRectangular in the existing AppProject/Widget target (StandBy free), reading wallet v2 per-currency balances via the same shared-store refresh-on-change plumbing as the shipped today-total widget (SharedSummary). fogWidth = deterministic f(days since last reconcile × typical cash-day spend from RhythmDetector history) — pure GoldengoCore code, no model. Tap deep-links into the shipped type-the-balance reconcile, claim prefilled, numpad focused; gap-to-Unaccounted creation already ships in IngestionStore+Wallet. Guess chip = heuristic score of the gap against Rhythm ghosts, weekday/hour history, and note-counter-payable amounts; max one guess above a confidence floor, rendered as an annotation on the existing Unaccounted entry. Unconfirmed guesses never count in totals; confirmed ones become reversible entries with 'guessed, confirmed' provenance. ATM lines in statement import raise the same prefilled reconcile card. Decimal math in memory, never in #Predicate. ~1.5 cycles.

**Curator:** Merge of Pocket Truth (surface and claim framing, kept) + Pocket Fog (the honesty band — the actual day-30 mechanic: the line changes even on silent days and blame points at the machine) + Named Gaps (guess chips at reconcile) + Squeeze-and-Settle's reconcile-on-import card. Strongest of the fifteen on the metric: the trigger is his hand in his wallet, not a notification.

**Day-30 verdict:** SURVIVES, but not for the reason the pitch claims. The pitch's load-bearing line — "fog is un-truth made visible, he cannot leave it alone" — is overclaimed and the simulation kills it by week 3. What actually survives is the other mechanic: the involuntary collision between hand-knowledge and screen-claim.

THE 30 DAYS, HONESTLY. Week 1: novelty carries it. He reconciles at setup, watches a rhythm-ghost lunch move the line before the phone is pocketed ("books track hands" — small real delight), burns his first fog on day 4, gets a correct guess chip, drop haptic. 3-4 reconciles. Week 2: reality. Day 9 fog hits ±800 mid-build-crunch; he ignores it. Nothing happens (correctly — design soul). Day 10, ±1,300, ignores again. Here the concept's hidden cost surfaces: typing the TRUE balance requires COUNTING the wallet — 8 notes plus ALL coins at a market stall is exactly the ceremony he hates. He starts deferring reconciles to free-count moments: post-ATM (machine just told him the amount) and near-empty wallet. Drops to 1-2 reconciles/week. Week 3 is the habituation test: fog becomes the STEADY STATE, and an itch successfully ignored ten times stops itching — this user demonstrably sat on verification tickets for a week, and fog is a verification ticket rendered on the lock screen. By day 17 the fog qua itch is dead. THE DAY THE DATA IS SLIGHTLY WRONG (day ~13, gave a friend 2,000 L, no ghost for that, fog too narrow to cover it): this is the concept's BEST day, not its worst — books-wrong is his only proven engagement spike (double-drain rant), the machine takes the blame, the fix is type-one-number, and a wrong guess chip costs one flick and never counts in totals. Unlike every guilt-based tracker, the wrong-data day strengthens trust here instead of ending it.

WHY IT STILL CLEARS THE DAY-30 BAR. The assassin's kill-rule is "depends on a habit it does not itself create" — and this concept's three legs all parasitize behavior that already happens involuntarily: (1) glances ride phone pickups (~100/day, structural, forever); (2) discrepancy DETECTION rides cash payments — he keeps his pocket number in his head, so auditing the claim is free, no count needed to notice it's off; a falsifiable-about-YOUR-pocket claim is the one widget genre that stays a read instead of becoming weather-widget wallpaper; (3) reconciliation rides ATM cycles — a cash-life Albanian must withdraw weekly, the statement-import card arrives prefilled, and post-ATM is the one moment the count is known for free. Day 30 equilibrium: 4-6 meaningful glances/day, ~2 event-anchored touches/week, zero willpower, zero remembered intentions. Verified against the repo: IngestionStore+Wallet (reconcile + gap-to-Unaccounted), RhythmDetector, SharedSummary, and the widget target all ship today; ~1.5 cycles is honest.

THE KILL-CONDITION TO WATCH (this is what would make survives=false, around day ~20): if ATM-anchored reconciles ALSO get dismissed (import happens at his desk hours after withdrawal, wallet in pocket — the "free count" is weaker than the sketch implies), fog saturates to ±half-his-wallet, the claim becomes meaningless, and a meaningless claim IS wallpaper. Three required guards: (1) cap fog width — past ~1 typical wallet's worth, degrade to plain words ("lost track · tap when wallet's out") rather than an absurd ±N that reads as broken; a claim must stay narrow enough to be falsifiable from memory or the free-audit leg dies; (2) the post-import reconcile card must persist quietly until a wallet-out moment (lock-screen tap) rather than demanding desk-time attention, or it inherits the verification-ticket fate; (3) currency-aware fog — EUR with no rhythm history must not fog ("losing track" of money that provably didn't move is a false confession and his correctness instinct will catch the app lying about its own blindness, which is the one unforgivable sin for this user). With those three guards, day 30 holds. Without guard 1 in particular, expect wallpaper-death at day 18-22.

**Friction verdict:** TRUE COST LEDGER. Notifications: zero — the only surfaces are passive (lock screen, StandBy), nothing ever interrupts or escalates. Required taps: zero — the steady state is a half-second glance on pickups he already makes ~100x/day; the concept parasitizes two existing behaviors (looking at his phone, handling cash) instead of manufacturing a new one. Decisions demanded: zero — the widget asserts, never asks; fog can be ignored forever with no badge, no red, no prompt. Memory burden: zero — the trigger is physical cash in his hand. Configuration: one-time lock-screen widget install (he is the maker; once, never again). Maintenance: zero — fogWidth is deterministic from reconcile-age × RhythmDetector history, no thresholds, no categories, no tuning. The voluntary path costs exactly his two proven-favorite verbs: type one number (numpad prefilled and focused via the already-shipped goldengo:// deep link + type-the-balance reconcile — verified in the repo: accessoryRectangular + widgetURL + onOpenURL all exist), then optionally judge one pre-written guess; ignoring the guess safely defaults to the shipped honest Unaccounted, so 'do nothing' is always a valid, non-punished outcome. No category picker, no note field, no 'what am I supposed to write there' — the chip pre-writes it. SOUL CHECK: no streaks, no scores; fog is true uncertainty made visible, not guilt — but this survives ONLY under three copy/behavior guardrails the sketch already commits to and must not slip: (1) blame stays on the machine ('losing track', never 'you haven't logged since Tue'), (2) fog never converts into a notification or any escalation, (3) max one confidence-floored guess, unconfirmed guesses never count in totals, confirmed ones are reversible with provenance — so no fabricated records. One watch-item: the ATM-import reconcile card must appear once per import and die on flick; if it re-appears on the next import for the same unresolved gap, that is a re-ask and he will churn. THE ONE REAL COST: reconciling means knowing what's in the wallet, i.e. counting — the thing he hates. It survives because the app never asks for it: the count is voluntary, timed to wallet-already-in-hand moments, mitigated by the shipped note counter, and it replaces a number he currently maintains in his head — so it is offloading existing cognitive work, not adding new work. Even the total-abandonment case fails gracefully: if he never reconciles, the widget degrades into an honestly-fogged estimate rather than a confident lie, which is precisely his 'systems that self-correct' preference — staleness is displayed, not hidden, so day-30 trust is structurally protected. Net: attention returned (a trusted answer to 'how much cash am I carrying', dozens of free involuntary audits daily, proof the books track reality — his only demonstrated engagement spike) decisively exceeds attention spent (zero marginal glances, ~1-2 voluntary reconciles/week). Cheapest concept possible for this user: nothing to remember, answer, configure, or maintain, and every offered verb is one he already loves.

## Lasts Until — runway on the lock screen

**Pitch:** He raises his phone and the lock screen says "Lasts until Oct 14 · −1 since yesterday." Overnight the hosting bill auto-captures and it reads "Oct 9 · −5" before he has touched anything. **Why him:** Budgets are fiction for lumpy freelance EUR+ALL income, but runway is the builder's native metric — he already thinks in it. A date is a fact, not a verdict: no guilt vector, nothing to answer. It moves daily with zero input because captures, imports, and Due ghosts land on their own; an Upwork payment is watching the date jump three weeks — his good news with zero ceremony. Falsifiable claims (his realized FX rate, earliest-dying currency) are the only kind of insight he respects, and every correction he makes anywhere makes his own number truer — the self-correcting property he loves. The ALL side running short cues the one money decision he actually makes — when to hit the ATM — and the withdrawal auto-captures on import, closing the loop with no input.

**Daily pull:** Every lock-screen raise — dozens a day — answers 'am I fine' in half a second via one date plus its overnight delta. Payday is the plot: the date leaps and he watches it the way he watches uptime. When the delta looks wrong it produces the one itch he reliably acts on — the books disagree with him — and the fix is one deep-link away. Day 30: it is the first thing his eye lands on at every unlock, because it is the only number that summarizes whether everything is fine.

**Build sketch:** Deterministic on-device math: trailing-28-day spend per currency from existing entries (outliers trimmed) minus upcoming Due ghosts (pendingSubscriptionCharges via SubscriptionSettlementPlanner), over wallet v2 + Provenance source balances; personal FX derived from his recorded exchange transfers (ExchangeRateCache as fallback only); lead with earliest-dying currency, per-currency split one tap away; read-only age-of-tranche metric extends ProvenanceAllocator (spends funded by Unaccounted excluded from the average, never guessed). Surfaces: accessoryInline, StandBy, one line on the shipped home widget via SharedSummary refresh-on-change; a 'how I got this' screen where every term links to its entries for one-tap correction. Pure functions tested against ProvenanceAllocatorTests fixtures; Decimal in memory post-fetch. One cycle.

**Curator:** Merge of Lasts Until (kept — a date in plain words beats an age number for a runway-brained builder) + Days Ahead (per-currency split, ATM-timing cue, age-of-money as the tap-deep detail; nearly free since FIFO is shipped) + The Teller's 'covered through' heartbeat.
